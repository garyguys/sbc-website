Member photos and logos.

Headshots: square JPEG, 600x600, named by member slug (e.g. karen-tyrell.jpg),
then set photo: '/members/<slug>.jpg' on the member in src/data/members.js.

Logos: PNG with a transparent or white background, in logos/, named by business
(e.g. logos/brella.png), then set logo: '/members/logos/<name>.png' on the member.
